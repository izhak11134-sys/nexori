Audience: international, English-speaking visitors.
Affiliate accounts: none yet. This is a selection plan, not a verified shopping catalog.
Keep the current site design and banner effects.

## Initial collection: 12 product slots

| Slots | Product direction | Visitor intent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–6 | One accessible display figure per world: One Piece, Naruto, Demon Slayer, Jujutsu Kaisen, Dragon Ball, Attack on Titan | Find a favorite character; start a collection |
| 7–8 | Two more detailed display figures from different worlds | Choose a centerpiece |

| 11 | Licensed T-shirt with a clear size chart | Wear a favorite fandom |
| 12 | Small collectible such as a keychain | Find a modest gift |

These are sourcing slots, not recommendations of specific SKUs. Start with in-stock products; add preorders only with clear release and cancellation information. Budget tiers should follow verified item and delivery costs rather than invented prices.

## Required record for each real listing

Record the exact product name, franchise, character, manufacturer, SKU, category, retailer, actual seller, product URL, approved affiliate URL, licensing evidence, permission/source for product images, dimensions/materials, target shipping destinations, observed price and currency, observation date, and availability/preorder status.

Do not use generated concept illustrations as photographs of real products. Do not publish authenticity, testing, stock, price, discount, or delivery claims without supporting evidence. Follow each affiliate program's requirements for images, pricing, disclosures, and links.

## Visitor experience once data is ready

- Find your universe: choose franchise, product category, and an optional budget. Explain that matches are editorial selections. Show broader recommendations when no exact matches exist.
- Shop an atmosphere: starter shelf, anime desk, and gifts, each backed by real listings.
- Product cards: actual permitted image, concise selection rationale, known specifications, save button, and “View at retailer.” Show price only through a permitted, maintainable source; otherwise show “Check price at retailer.”
- Keep existing saved finds and guides. Add a clear affiliate disclosure near outbound recommendations.
